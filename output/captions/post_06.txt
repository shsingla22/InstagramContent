From Horse Harnesses to $200 Billion: The Hermès Story

In 1837, Thierry Hermès opened a Paris workshop making horse harnesses. When automobiles replaced horses, his family didn't panic — they turned their legendary leather skills toward handbags. The Kelly bag? Originally a saddle carrier. Each one takes 18-24 hours of hand-stitching using the same saddle stitch technique from the original harnesses. The Birkin? 48 hours per piece. Some sell for $400,000+. Today Hermès is worth over $200 billion, but the horse remains at its heart.

"The horse is the heart of Hermès. It lives in every stitch, every buckle, every piece of leather we touch."
